Privacy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Data collection

This informational site has no accounts, advertising or forms. Each page load adds one aggregate view without saving a persistent visitor identifier. If you press the appreciation control, the browser creates a random value for this site and saves it in local storage so it can remember your progress and enforce the 20-press limit. The counter stores only salted one-way identifiers, the site name, reaction total, short-lived rate-limit records and timestamps. It does not use the values for advertising or cross-site profiling.

Your choices

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals prevent the automatic view record. Pressing the appreciation control is an explicit request to save that reaction. Counter details are available in the shared privacy policy.

External links

Links to GitHub, Instagram, email and project sites are controlled by those services. Their own privacy terms apply when you visit them.

Hosting

AntiDeploy and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary request information needed to deliver the site, such as IP addresses, timestamps and browser details. Contact kansagara.dwij@gmail.com with a privacy question.
