Privacy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Data collection

This informational site has no accounts, advertising or forms. Its visit and appreciation panel uses Dwij's portfolio counter. The counter converts the network address and browser user-agent already included in a normal request into a salted, one-way identifier. It stores only that identifier, the site name, reaction total and timestamps. It does not store the source network address, set cookies, write a counter identifier to browser storage or use the result for profiling.

Your choices

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals prevent the automatic visit record. Pressing the appreciation control is an explicit request to save that reaction. Counter details are available in the shared privacy policy.

External links

Links to GitHub, Instagram, email and project sites are controlled by those services. Their own privacy terms apply when you visit them.

Hosting

AntiDeploy and its infrastructure providers may process ordinary request information needed to deliver the site, such as IP addresses, timestamps and browser details. Contact kansagara.dwij@gmail.com with a privacy question.
