Cookies

Last updated: 28 September 2026

No first-party cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. The view counter stores no visitor identifier. If you press the appreciation control, a random first-party value is saved in local storage to remember that browser's progress and enforce the 20-press limit. No optional analytics, advertising or third-party tracking is loaded.

Counter request

The page requests its counter script and totals from dwij-signal.vercel.app. Its limited data handling is described in the Privacy Policy.

Linked services

Following an external link takes you to another service that may use cookies under its own policy.

Changes

If tracking or cookie-based features are introduced, this notice and the consent experience will be updated before those features are enabled.
